How to Play
Got it
Wonder World: Beta
Your goal for now: dig deep, find Gold, craft a full set of Gold Armor (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots), and make it back to the surface alive.
Moving
  • WASD move
  • Mouse look around
  • Space jump, swim up
  • Shift sprint
  • Ctrl or Q sneak, go down
  • V see yourself from behind, from in front, or through your own eyes
Using things
  • Left-click mine blocks and attack
  • Right-click use what you hold: place a block, eat, draw a bow, or open a station (crafting table, oven, smelter, chest)
  • Stuck with your head in a block? Just mine — it digs out the block at your head first
Menus
  • Esc close a menu, or pause
  • Click the screen to take hold of the mouse again
With a controller
  • Left stick move (click it to sprint)  ·  Right stick look
  • A jump  ·  B sneak  ·  RT mine and attack  ·  LT place and use
  • LB RB hotbar  ·  Y inventory  ·  X crafting  ·  Start pause
  • In menus: D-pad to move, A to choose, B to go back. The full list is in Settings, under Controls.
Playing together
  • On this screen: pause, open Players, and press A on another controller. Up to four, each with their own part of the screen
  • On your network: pause, open Players and turn on Open to the network. The others choose Play, then Join a game
Inventory & crafting
  • E inventory  ·  C crafting, or the creative menu
  • 1 – 0 pick a hotbar slot
  • Crafting Table tools, armor and blocks  ·  Oven cooks food  ·  Smelter ingots and glass
  • Mine ore, smelt it into ingots, then craft armor and tools
Staying alive
  • Keep an eye on Health, Hunger and Energy
  • Hold Right-click with food to eat (watch the bar)
  • Don't suffocate inside blocks, and beware long falls in deep caves
  • Gold is near the very bottom of the world — pack food before you dig
